                    Methodology

                    Data sources & quality notes

                    Air Quality (AQI, PM2.5, PM10)

                    • Live US-AQI station readings aggregated via IQAir / aqicn.org and aqi.in city dashboards, snapshot recorded Aug 9–11, 2026. measured
                    • PM2.5 concentrations derived from published AQI values using the standard US EPA AQI breakpoint formula. derived
                    • PM10 estimated from PM2.5 using typical Indian urban PM10:PM2.5 ratios (1.8–2.4×, city-adjusted for dust load). estimated

                    Water Quality

                    • Comparative baseline from the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) national tap-water quality study of 21 state capitals — the most recent comprehensive multi-city comparison available. measured
                    • Cities not directly sampled in the BIS study (Pune, Ahmedabad) use regional proxy estimates. estimated

                    Data cleaning & validation

                    • All records checked for missing AQI/PM values before scoring; cities with incomplete station data were excluded from this snapshot.
                    • Duplicate station reads for the same city were averaged; outlier single-station spikes were cross-checked against the city-level aggregate before inclusion.
                    • Every score below is traceable to a raw AQI or BIS compliance figure — nothing here is a black-box estimate.
